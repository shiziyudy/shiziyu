DATA PRIVACY & INTERNAL USE

Privacy Policy.

This policy describes how Dongying Shiziyu E-Commerce Co., Ltd. (东营狮子鱼电子商务有限公司) intends to handle Amazon information in connection with its private, internal Selling Partner API application.

Last updated: November 19, 2026Internal application · Not a public seller service

01. Scope and our role

We operate our own Amazon seller account and are planning a private application for authorized internal business workflows. The application is not available for public registration and is not offered to independent Amazon sellers or other external businesses.

This policy covers the information processed by that internal application and the limited information involved in visiting this informational website.

02. Information the application may process

Subject to the permissions actually granted and the features implemented, our internal application may retrieve information associated with our authorized Amazon seller account, including:

  • Product listings, SKU identifiers, listing status and pricing information.
  • Inventory balances, order status, sales quantities, and fulfillment or FBA shipment data.
  • Settlement reports, transaction summaries, fees and sales performance metrics.
  • Authentication tokens and technical records needed to maintain secure authorized access.

Our initial planned use cases do not require restricted access to Amazon buyer personally identifiable information (PII). We would review any additional data access separately before expanding the application.

03. Why we use the information

The application is intended to support inventory planning, product administration, order monitoring, pricing review, financial reconciliation, performance analysis, and internal reporting for our own Amazon business. It is not designed for selling Amazon information, third-party marketing or unrelated commercial purposes.

04. External parties and infrastructure

The initial development and operation are planned to take place on a company-controlled local computer. In the future, we may use Tencent Cloud or Alibaba Cloud for hosting, storage, scheduled processing and related infrastructure. Before doing so, we intend to review applicable data protection obligations, service-provider arrangements and security safeguards.

We do not offer Amazon seller information or API access to external sellers. Any future infrastructure provider would act only in a supporting capacity for our internal systems, subject to applicable contractual and security requirements. Actual infrastructure choices may change.

05. Security approach

We intend to apply safeguards appropriate to our implementation and Amazon's applicable requirements, including authorization controls, secure storage of credentials, encrypted data transmission, restricted access, security monitoring and incident-response procedures. The specific controls in use depend on the development and deployment stage and are assessed before production operation.

API credentials and access tokens must not be published in website files, public code repositories or client-side scripts.

06. Retention and deletion

Our approach is to retain Amazon information only while reasonably necessary for legitimate internal business operations, applicable legal obligations and Amazon's data protection requirements. Data that is no longer needed should be deleted or otherwise handled in accordance with those requirements. Retention periods may vary by data category and applicable policy.

07. Visits to this website

This is a static informational website. It does not contain account registration, payment forms or an API authorization flow, and we do not intentionally collect visitor submissions through this website. The website hosting provider may process routine technical request information under its own policies. We do not embed third-party analytics trackers in the files supplied for this website.

08. Contact and policy questions

For questions about this privacy policy or our company's handling of information, contact us at shiziyudy@126.com or by telephone at +86 186 7862 2290.
